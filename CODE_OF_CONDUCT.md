# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, technical, and free of harassment or discrimination. Assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and keep security disclosures private until they are resolved.

Reports about conduct can be sent privately to the maintainer through the contact route on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.

The full Contributor Covenant 2.1 text is available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
